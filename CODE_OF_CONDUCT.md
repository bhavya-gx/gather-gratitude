# Community expectations

Be kind, specific, and respectful. Welcome different backgrounds, abilities, family structures, beliefs, and experiences of holidays. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose someone’s personal information, or pressure people to share private reflections.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Report concerns privately through an available maintainer contact; do not publish personal information in an issue. Critique code and ideas constructively.
