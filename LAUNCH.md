# Thanksgiving gratitude series

Core idea: **Notice the good before Thanksgiving arrives.**

Use original or licensed photos, short readable text, and real app screenshots containing explicitly fictional sample entries. Do not reuse Aya’s footage, branding, or unverified revenue claims. One slideshow daily is a starting cadence, not a promised growth formula.

## Lead-up: November 10–22, 2026

Rotate ordinary comforts, friendship, personal growth, a place to land, long-distance connection, and small acts of care.

Five-slide template:
1. How to make this Thanksgiving feel more meaningful.
2. Find one photo from this year that makes you smile.
3. Open Gather. Write what was happening—and why it mattered.
4. Keep one little good each day until Thanksgiving.
5. On Thanksgiving, reread the moments you saved.

## November 23–25

1. Before Thanksgiving gets busy, think of one person who made this year easier.
2. Remember one specific thing they did.
3. Write it in Gather so you can find the words.
4. “When you ___, it helped me ___.”
5. Send them that message tonight.

## Thanksgiving Day: November 26, 2026

1. Before today becomes a blur, take one minute.
2. Notice one small thing you’re glad is here.
3. Open Gather: “Today, I’m grateful for ___ because ___.”
4. If a person comes to mind, tell them.
5. Tonight, save one moment you want to remember.

Include people working, alone, far from home, or having a difficult holiday. Gratitude should invite reflection without requiring happiness. Share the deployed app’s HTTPS URL and the GitHub source link. Gather has no built-in analytics; evaluate public-post engagement without collecting private journal entries.
